Does every package include installation?

Yes. Free installation is included in all four Candy Craze packages.

Do Basic and Standard include candy?

No. Basic Craze and Standard Craze are setup-only packages and do not include candies or snacks.

Which packages include candy and snacks?

Popular Craze includes 6 candies + 3 snacks. Premium Craze includes 9 candies + 3 snacks. The selections are random.

Which packages include the 3-tier rack?

Standard Craze and Premium Craze include the 3-tier rack.
