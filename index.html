Candy wall FAQ

Good to know.

Quick answers about Candy Craze packages, random treats, delivery and booking.

Does every package include installation?

Yes. Free installation is included in all four Candy Craze packages.

Do Basic and Standard include candies or snacks?

No. Basic Craze and Standard Craze are setup-only packages. If you want candies or snacks included, choose Popular Craze or Premium Craze.

Which packages include candies and snacks?

Popular Craze includes 6 candies + 3 snacks. Premium Craze includes 9 candies + 3 snacks. The selections are random and subject to availability.

Can I choose the add-on candy or snack brands?

No. Add-ons are only for Popular Craze and Premium Craze. You choose the quantity bundle while Candy Craze selects the actual brands randomly based on available stock.

Which packages include the 3-tier rack?

Standard Craze and Premium Craze include the 3-tier rack.

Is delivery free?

Free delivery is available within 10km.

How do I book?

Choose your package, select eligible add-ons if required, fill in your event date and location, then tap “Send via WhatsApp”.
